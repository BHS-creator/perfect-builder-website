Building Contractor FAQs – Sheffield

Useful answers about extensions, renovations, structural work, approvals, project timescales and construction services.

How much does a house extension cost in Sheffield?

The cost of a house extension depends on the size, specification, structural requirements, finishes, access and approvals required. Perfect Building Contractor Ltd can assess the proposed work and provide a project-specific quotation.

Do I need planning permission for a house extension in Sheffield?

Some extensions may fall within permitted development rights while others require planning permission. The requirements depend on the property and proposed work. Check the current rules and obtain the appropriate approvals before construction.

Do I need Building Regulations approval for an extension?

Most substantial building work and structural alterations are subject to Building Regulations requirements. The exact submission and inspection route depends on the work, so the project should be assessed before construction starts.

How long does a house extension take?

Timescales vary with the size and complexity of the project, structural work, approvals, materials, site access and finishes. A realistic programme should be agreed after the scope has been assessed.

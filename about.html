About Our Company

Professional Builders in Sheffield

Perfect Building Contractor LTD is a professional building and construction company providing residential and commercial building services in Sheffield and surrounding areas. We work with homeowners, property owners and businesses on a wide range of construction, renovation and property improvement projects.

With more than 30 years of experience and over 300 completed projects, we bring practical experience, careful workmanship and a professional approach to every project. Our services include house extensions, renovations, loft conversions, garage conversions, roofing, refurbishment, building alterations and new build projects.

We are proud to operate as a VAT-registered UK building business, providing clear and professional quotations and invoices for our services.

Planning Permission and House Extensions in Sheffield

Planning requirements depend on the proposed work and property. Homeowners should check current planning rules before starting construction.

What Should You Check?

Start by defining the work you want to complete and gather any available drawings, measurements, photographs or specifications. Ask builders to explain what is included and excluded from their quotation.

Understand Your Proposed Extension

Before construction begins, it is helpful to clearly define the proposed extension, including its approximate size, position, layout and intended use. The requirements that may apply can depend on the individual property and the nature of the proposed work.

Planning Permission and Other Requirements

Planning permission requirements can vary depending on the property, location and scope of the proposed extension. Other approvals or technical requirements may also apply to a project. Homeowners should check the relevant current requirements before starting construction.

Building Regulations and Structural Requirements

Building Regulations, structural design and other technical requirements may apply depending on the proposed work. Matters such as structure, insulation, drainage, ventilation, fire safety and other technical elements may need to be considered as part of a project.

Appropriate professional and local-authority advice should be obtained where required.
